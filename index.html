Magnet Dash — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Magnet Dash” (bundle ID com.tanmay.magnetrush)

Magnet Dash is a free game. We keep it simple: we do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts. This page explains the small amount of data the app does handle, and why.

1. Game progress (stored on your device only)

Your coins, upgrades, unlocked skins, high score, settings, and daily-reward streak are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. They never leave your device unless you use Game Center (see below). Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Magnet Dash shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for a revive, extra coins, or a skin) and occasional interstitials between runs. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version, coarse location derived from IP address);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

If you tap Ask App Not to Track, Google still serves ads but they are not personalized to you across other apps. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU/UK you will also see a consent choice before ads load.

Google’s handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from AdMob apps. Buying Remove Ads stops all interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain available if you choose them.

3. Purchases (Apple)

In-app purchases (coin packs, premium skins, Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your high score and achievements are submitted to Apple’s Game Center leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. This is controlled by Apple and can be turned off in the app’s Settings or in iOS Settings → Game Center.

5. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-up, or login.
  • No collection of name, email, phone, photos, contacts, or precise location.
  • No selling of personal data.
  • No analytics SDK beyond what AdMob itself reports.

6. Children

Magnet Dash is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob is configured to follow applicable child-privacy rules.

7. Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalized ads: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and Google’s Ad Settings.
  • Delete all game data: delete the app.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date.
