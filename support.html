How to play

Drag your thumb left and right. Your magnet pulls in any metal that comes within range. Collect quickly to build a combo, and survive as long as you can — the world keeps speeding up.

Common questions

Some objects won't come to me. Heavy items (metal blocks, toasters, safes) need a stronger magnet. Upgrade Magnet Power. If an object jiggles and turns red, it's too heavy for your current magnet.

My magnet stopped working. You either hit a wooden crate (non-metal junk jams the magnet for about 1.5 seconds) or you're inside a red No Magnet Zone. Drag away from the red field — it also pulls you in, and it will capture you if you stay.

I bought something and didn't get it. Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases on the Coins tab. If it still doesn't appear, email me with the date of purchase and I'll sort it out.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device and survives app updates, but it is deleted if you delete the app. There is no account, so progress can't be transferred between devices.

How do I remove ads? Shop → Coins tab → Remove Ads. This removes interstitial ads permanently. Rewarded videos stay available if you choose to watch them.

How do I turn off sound or haptics? Settings from the main menu, or the buttons on the pause screen.
